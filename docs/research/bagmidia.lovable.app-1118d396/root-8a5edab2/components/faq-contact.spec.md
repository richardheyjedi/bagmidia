# FAQ and contact specification

- Targets: `#faq` and `#contato`.
- Interaction model: single-open FAQ accordion, role pill toggle, WhatsApp form submit.
- FAQ card: rounded 2xl, border, card background, 20px horizontal padding; chevron rotates via original data-state selector.
- Contact: two-column desktop layout; WhatsApp and email cards on left; rounded 3xl form card on right; two-column inputs on `sm`.
- Form fields: Nome, Empresa, Telefone/WhatsApp, E-mail, Mensagem. Privacy copy states that data is not stored.
